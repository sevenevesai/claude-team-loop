# Write-up pack

The article and its three visuals, ready to post.

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `writeup.md` | the article, under 4,000 characters including its Markdown |
| `img/the-loop.png` | the big picture: five stations around the plugin, drawn as a plant that grows |
| `img/what-travels.png` | what stays on each person's machine, what travels, what comes back |
| `img/signals-to-upgrades.png` | eight things people say at their screens and the upgrade each becomes |
| `img/*.svg` | the sources of the three images |

The images are 1600 pixels wide and hand-drawn in style. The SVGs use the Ink Free typeface and
fall back to another handwriting face where it is missing, so post the PNGs. To change one, edit
its SVG and export it again at the size in its `width` and `height`.

## Alt text

- The loop: "A hand-drawn loop of five stations around a potted plant labelled the plugin: work,
  notice, relay, review, release. The team owns the first three, the plugin's owner the last two.
  The plant's leaves read skills, agreements, gates, hooks, integrations and shared stuff."
- What travels: "Notebooks labelled session history, project notes and local overrides stay on
  your own machine. A paper plane passes a tick marked 'you approve first' and lands on a pin
  board of cards with statuses. On the owner's desk: nightly review, backlog, plugin fix and a
  parcel. A long arrow returns: a status on every card and the fix in your next session."
- Signals to upgrades: "Eight sticky notes with things people say while working, each stamped
  with what it becomes: new skill, skill fix, better trigger, working agreement, gate, hook,
  integration, shared resource."
